### 🦋 Butterfly Effect of Success (Progress)

**Brief:** Small initial successes, fueled by **persistence**, act as a catalyst, sparking a **chain reaction** of progress across all other areas of life.

A small initial success, achieved through **strong determination** and **persistence**, acts as a **catalyst**, triggering a chain reaction of **progresses in other areas**. Conversely, even one small deviation that **breaks your plan** can cause you to **lose momentum** and the power to achieve your ultimate target.

### 🛑 The Cost of Deviation (Discipline)

**Brief:** Maintaining discipline is paramount, as a single **small deviation** from the plan can **break momentum** and forfeit the power needed to reach the target.

**A small step that breaks your plan** is highly detrimental, as it causes you to **lose the momentum** and the **power** required to achieve your target.

**Religion** is a **social operating system** built on a core belief. It establishes **behavioral protocols** that ensure **group cohesion**, with its quality proven solely by its **long-term robustness** and **evolutionary success**.

### 🤝 Attracting Societal Support (Goals)

**Brief:** While we cannot fully determine our future due to reliance on **societal support**, we can **attract** the necessary resources by demonstrating we are the **most suitable candidate**.

We can’t fully determine our future because we **rely on support from society**, but we can actively **attract** that support to move toward our goals. The universe only provides support to **selected people** who are the **most suitable candidates**, making **passion and aptitude** essential for securing the necessary education and career platforms. Even a genius needs the right **platform** to prevent their talents from fading.

### 💻 The Essence of C vs. Rust (Software)

**Brief:** C’s **type compatibility** (treating types like `enum` or `char` as `int`) allows programmers to focus on the **essence** of data, a **primitive beauty** that modern languages like Rust currently lack, suggesting a full C replacement is not yet here.

C has **type compatibility**—no matter the type (e.g., `enum`, `char`), the compiler considers them compatible with `int`. This helps us consider them in their **essence** and program without compiler disturbance. Rust stepped away from this **primitive beauty**, lacking the focus on **understanding the essence**. Therefore, a language that can fully replace C is not yet here.

### 💡 Mindset Shift (Life)

**Brief:** Shift from the unproductive, complaint-driven **'What should be, but...'** (reactive mindset) to the proactive, solution-oriented **'What is..., it can be...if...'** (correct mindset) to find opportunities for change.

The **wrong mindset** often starts with **'What should be, but...'** and that usually leads to **complaining**, which is unproductive. The **correct mindset**, however, is **'What is..., it can be...if...'**. This crucial shift helps you **understand the situation** and gives you the **opportunity to actively change it**.

### 🎁 Setbacks as Growth (Life)

**Brief:** **Every setback is more valuable than a success** because it clearly **exposes our weaknesses** and drives genuine growth, prompting us to be **grateful** for the difficulty and everyone involved.

I truly believe that **every setback is more valuable than a success**, because setbacks are what truly **expose our weaknesses** and force us to grow. Therefore, we should be **grateful for those difficult experiences**, and we should also appreciate the people who played a role in that situation.

### 🗡️ Creativity: Risk vs. Reward (Life)

**Brief:** **Creativity** is a **double-edged sword** that is only rewarding when coupled with **talent and determination**; otherwise, it is wiser to be **conservative** and **emulate the steps of successful people**.

I see **creativity** as a **double-edged sword**—it's **both highly rewarding and inherently risky**. Only when someone is genuinely **talented and determined** should they pursue a creative career. In most other cases, it is more sensible to be **conservative** and **follow the steps of successful people** to avoid insurmountable obstacles.

### 🤝 "saint" and "sinner" (Life) 
**Brief:** Discard the lazy labels of "saint" and "sinner." Judge instead by connection: are we collaborative, kind, and patient?

On the one hand, tension arises when the goal eclipses the person. Task-driven focus can seem cold, while people-driven focus can stall progress. The art of cooperation lies in the middle ground. We must learn to pursue our targets fiercely without forgetting to treat the people helping us gently.

On the other hand, conflict is simply the gap between the target and the current reality. Since targets and situations are always changing, this gap is inherent. Consequently, the way we manage this gap drives the evolution of the situation. I believe we should actively maintain the gap at a controllable level to strike **a balance between flexibility and smoothness**. It is crucial to monitor this gap to see how we can narrow or enlarge it to drive progress.
