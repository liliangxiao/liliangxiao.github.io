# High efficiency discussion
## High efficiency two-people communication
In highly effective two-person communication, it's a constant loop of understanding and response, much like playing ping-pong. You don't just receive the ball; you have to hit it back. The discussion only ends when one side either 'wins the point' or the other 'drops the ball.' A discussion is successful if the person with the soundest argument prevails—that's a fair result. If one person can't express themselves well because their communication skills aren't on the same level as the other, they can feel defeated. This often leads to either giving up ('losing') or turning the conversation into an emotional argument, at which point the communication fails.

To successfully 'hit back the ball', we need to 
- comprehend the words of the other,
- compare with our thoughs and find the gap
- find out the cause of the gap
- organize our words

## High efficiency three-people discussion
In a three-person communication, you may need to imaging a three people ping-pong game. **For every expression, two people can cooperate to give a feedback.** It is more effective than two-people communication. However, **two people can also give different feedback** and in this case, if we need to comprehend the discussion as a listener, we could face heavy load of memory issue during the process of two-ideas feedback loop.

To successfully comprehend the discussion, we need to 
- comprehend the starting "ball" to understand what's on the table for discussion
- **record/take note of** each feedback to the "ball"(positive/negative and reason)
- identify final agreement

It includes the skill of listening an article that may have different opinions.(For each opinion: positive/negive and reasons)
